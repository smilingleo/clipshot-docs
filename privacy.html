Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Overview

ClipShot is a macOS screenshot, screen recording, and scroll capture utility that runs entirely on your Mac. Your privacy is straightforward: ClipShot does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

ClipShot does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reporting
  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No network requests (except StoreKit transactions handled by Apple)
  • No advertising or tracking

Captured Content

Screenshots, recordings, and scroll captures are created and stored locally on your Mac. ClipShot does not upload, analyze, or share your captured content. Files remain under your control — they are only saved or copied to the clipboard when you explicitly choose to do so.

In-App Purchases

ClipShot uses Apple's StoreKit framework for the free trial and full version purchase. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. ClipShot does not receive or store any payment information.

Permissions

ClipShot requests the following macOS permissions:

  • Screen Recording — required to capture the screen region you select.
  • Accessibility (optional) — used only for scroll capture, where the app sends scroll events to the active window.

These permissions are used solely for their stated purpose and do not enable any data collection.

Local Storage

ClipShot stores user preferences (hotkey settings, launch-at-login) in a local configuration file (~/.config/clipshot/config.ini) and license state in macOS NSUserDefaults. No data leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

ClipShot does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Since ClipShot does not collect data, material changes are unlikely.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us via the GitHub Issues page.
